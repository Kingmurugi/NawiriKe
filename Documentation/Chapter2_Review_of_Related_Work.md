# CHAPTER 2: REVIEW OF RELATED WORK

## 2.1 Introduction

This chapter presents a comprehensive review of existing literature and related systems in the field of community support management and Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems. The review examines the historical development of community support systems, analyzes existing prototypes and platforms, identifies emerging trends, and highlights the research gap that this project addresses. The review encompasses both global and local case studies to provide a contextual understanding of the current state of community support management systems.

## 2.2 Research Methodology of Literature Review

The literature review for this project was conducted using a systematic approach involving multiple academic databases including IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar, and institutional repositories. The search strategy employed keywords such as "community support management," "donation management systems," "CRM for non-profits," "humanitarian aid technology," "mobile money integration," and "charitable platform design." Both peer-reviewed academic journals and industry reports were included to ensure a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.

The review prioritized sources published between 2015 and 2024 to ensure relevance to current technological capabilities and practices. Sources were evaluated based on their credibility, relevance to the research topic, and contribution to understanding community support management systems. The review process involved reading abstracts, selecting relevant articles, and synthesizing key findings to identify patterns, trends, and gaps in existing research.

## 2.3 History of the Research Topic

The concept of community support management has evolved significantly over the past few decades, transitioning from traditional manual processes to increasingly sophisticated digital solutions. In the early 2000s, community support initiatives primarily relied on paper-based record keeping, manual donation processing, and informal networks for connecting donors with recipients (Williams, 2005). These approaches were characterized by limited transparency, inefficient processes, and challenges in tracking the impact of donations.

The advent of web technologies in the mid-2000s marked a significant turning point in community support management. Platforms such as GoFundMe (launched in 2010) and Kickstarter (launched in 2009) demonstrated the potential of online platforms to facilitate donations and crowdfunding for various causes (Chen et al., 2018). These platforms introduced features such as online donation processing, donor profiles, and progress tracking, setting the foundation for modern community support management systems.

The proliferation of mobile money services, particularly in developing countries, has further transformed the landscape of community support management. M-Pesa, launched in Kenya in 2007, achieved widespread adoption and demonstrated the potential of mobile money to facilitate financial transactions in regions with limited traditional banking infrastructure (Jack & Suri, 2011). This development created new opportunities for integrating mobile payment capabilities into community support platforms.

Recent years have seen the emergence of CRM-based approaches to community support management, adapting proven business management principles to the humanitarian sector. These systems emphasize donor relationship management, comprehensive reporting, and data-driven decision making (Thompson & Davis, 2020). The integration of advanced analytics, artificial intelligence, and blockchain technology represents the current frontier in community support management system development.

## 2.4 Review of Related Prototypes and Systems

### 2.4.1 Global Case Studies

**GoFundMe**

GoFundMe is one of the world's largest crowdfunding platforms, launched in 2010, that enables individuals to raise funds for various causes including medical expenses, education, emergencies, and community projects (GoFundMe, 2023). The platform features user-friendly campaign creation tools, social sharing capabilities, real-time donation tracking, and transparent reporting. According to Johnson and Smith (2022), GoFundMe has facilitated over $17 billion in donations since its inception, demonstrating the significant potential of online donation platforms.

The platform's strengths include its intuitive user interface, social media integration, and comprehensive donor communication tools. However, GoFundMe primarily focuses on individual campaigns rather than ongoing community support management, and lacks features for victim application management, fund distribution tracking, and administrative oversight that are essential for organized community support initiatives.

**DonorsChoose**

DonorsChoose is a United States-based platform that connects public school teachers with donors who wish to fund classroom projects (DonorsChoose, 2023). Launched in 2000, the platform has facilitated over $1.5 billion in donations to public schools. The system features project proposal submission, donor selection of specific projects, fulfillment tracking, and impact reporting.

The platform's approach to transparency and donor engagement has been widely praised. Donors can see exactly how their contributions are being used and receive updates on project outcomes. However, DonorsChoose is specifically designed for educational contexts and does not address broader community support needs. Additionally, the platform does not include features for victim application management or general fund distribution mechanisms.

**GlobalGiving**

GlobalGiving is a nonprofit organization that connects donors with grassroots projects around the world (GlobalGiving, 2023). The platform features project vetting processes, donation tracking, impact reporting, and donor engagement tools. According to their annual report, GlobalGiving has facilitated over $600 million in donations to more than 27,000 projects in 170 countries.

The platform's strength lies in its rigorous project vetting process and comprehensive impact reporting. However, GlobalGiving primarily focuses on connecting donors with nonprofit organizations rather than direct individual-to-individual support. The system lacks features for managing individual victim applications, direct donor-to-victim connections, and real-time fund distribution tracking.

**Kiva**

Kiva is a nonprofit organization that enables individuals to lend money to low-income entrepreneurs and students in over 80 countries (Kiva, 2023). Launched in 2005, Kiva has facilitated over $1.8 billion in loans. The platform features borrower profiles, lender selection, repayment tracking, and social impact reporting.

Kiva's innovative approach to micro-lending has demonstrated the potential of technology to facilitate financial inclusion. However, Kiva focuses specifically on loans rather than donations, and the repayment model differs from the donation-based approach of community support initiatives. Additionally, the platform does not include features for managing victim applications or general fund distribution.

**Charity Navigator**

Charity Navigator is an independent charity evaluator that provides ratings and information about nonprofit organizations to help donors make informed decisions (Charity Navigator, 2023). While not a donation platform itself, Charity Navigator's rating system has influenced transparency and accountability practices in the nonprofit sector.

The platform's emphasis on financial transparency and accountability is relevant to community support management. However, Charity Navigator focuses on evaluating existing organizations rather than facilitating donations or managing community support processes directly.

### 2.4.2 Local Case Studies

**M-Changa**

M-Changa is a Kenyan-based mobile fundraising platform that enables individuals and organizations to raise funds through mobile money (M-Changa, 2023). Launched in 2012, the platform has facilitated over KES 5 billion in donations. The system features SMS-based donation processing, real-time tracking, and social sharing capabilities.

M-Changa's integration with M-Pesa and other mobile money services makes it particularly relevant to the Kenyan context. The platform demonstrates the potential of mobile-first approaches to donation management. However, M-Changa primarily focuses on campaign-based fundraising rather than ongoing community support management, and lacks features for victim application management and systematic fund distribution.

**GiveDirectly**

GiveDirectly is a nonprofit organization that provides unconditional cash transfers to people living in extreme poverty, primarily in Kenya and Uganda (GiveDirectly, 2023). The organization uses mobile money to distribute funds directly to recipients, emphasizing transparency and efficiency in aid delivery.

GiveDirectly's approach to direct cash transfers is innovative and has demonstrated positive impacts on recipient welfare. However, the organization operates as a direct aid provider rather than a platform for connecting multiple donors with multiple recipients. The system does not include features for donor selection of recipients, donation tracking, or comprehensive reporting for individual donors.

**Safaricom Foundation**

The Safaricom Foundation, the corporate social responsibility arm of Safaricom (the company behind M-Pesa), implements various community support initiatives across Kenya (Safaricom Foundation, 2023). The foundation's programs include education support, health initiatives, and disaster response.

The foundation's work demonstrates the potential of corporate involvement in community support. However, the foundation operates as a direct implementer rather than a platform for broader community engagement. The system does not provide features for individual donors to contribute or track their donations.

**Kenya Red Cross**

The Kenya Red Cross Society implements humanitarian assistance programs across the country, including disaster response, health services, and community support initiatives (Kenya Red Cross, 2023). The organization has developed digital systems for volunteer management and disaster response coordination.

The Kenya Red Cross's experience in humanitarian aid delivery is valuable for understanding community support needs. However, the organization's systems are primarily focused on disaster response rather than ongoing community support management, and do not include features for individual donor engagement or direct donor-to-recipient connections.

**Local Church and Community Organization Initiatives**

Various local churches and community organizations in Kenya implement community support programs using manual processes and basic digital tools (Personal observation, 2023). These initiatives often involve collecting donations from members and distributing them to individuals in need within the community.

These local initiatives demonstrate the ongoing need for community support and the willingness of community members to contribute. However, the manual processes used are characterized by inefficiencies, lack of transparency, and limited tracking capabilities. The absence of systematic approaches to donor management, victim verification, and fund distribution highlights the need for improved systems.

## 2.5 Emerging Trends and Patterns

Several emerging trends and patterns have been identified in the field of community support management systems:

**Mobile-First Design:** Given the widespread adoption of mobile phones and mobile money services in developing countries, successful community support platforms are increasingly adopting mobile-first design approaches (Mutua & Kimani, 2022). This includes SMS-based donation processing, mobile-optimized interfaces, and integration with local mobile money services.

**Transparency and Accountability:** Donors increasingly demand transparency in how their contributions are used (Brown et al., 2021). Modern platforms are responding by providing real-time tracking, detailed impact reporting, and verification of recipient information. Blockchain technology is being explored by some platforms to enhance transparency further.

**Data-Driven Decision Making:** The collection and analysis of comprehensive data on donations, distributions, and impact is becoming increasingly important (Thompson & Davis, 2020). Advanced analytics and machine learning are being used to identify trends, optimize resource allocation, and measure impact more effectively.

**Social Media Integration:** Successful platforms leverage social media for donor acquisition, campaign promotion, and community building (Chen et al., 2018). Social sharing features, influencer partnerships, and viral marketing approaches are becoming standard practices.

**Personalization and Donor Engagement:** Platforms are increasingly focusing on personalized donor experiences, including targeted communications, customized donation suggestions, and recognition programs (Johnson & Smith, 2022). These approaches aim to increase donor retention and lifetime value.

**Integration with Multiple Payment Methods:** To maximize accessibility, platforms are integrating with various payment methods including credit cards, mobile money, bank transfers, and cryptocurrencies (Williams, 2023). This multi-channel approach accommodates diverse donor preferences.

**Emphasis on User Experience:** User experience design has become a critical success factor for community support platforms (Anderson & Lee, 2021). Intuitive interfaces, streamlined processes, and responsive design are essential for attracting and retaining users.

## 2.6 Research Gap

Despite the existence of various donation platforms and community support initiatives, several gaps remain that this project addresses:

**Lack of Comprehensive Community Support Management:** Existing platforms primarily focus on either crowdfunding campaigns or direct aid delivery, but do not provide comprehensive systems for managing ongoing community support initiatives including victim application management, fund distribution, and administrative oversight.

**Limited Local Context Adaptation:** Many global platforms are not adapted to local contexts, particularly in terms of mobile money integration, local payment infrastructure, and community-specific needs. There is a need for systems designed specifically for the Kenyan context.

**Absence of CRM-Based Approaches:** While CRM systems are widely used in business contexts, their application to community support management remains limited. The adaptation of CRM principles to community support presents an opportunity for improved donor relationship management and systematic approach to community engagement.

**Limited Real-Time Tracking and Reporting:** Many existing systems lack comprehensive real-time tracking and reporting capabilities that enable donors to monitor their donations and administrators to make data-driven decisions. Enhanced transparency features are needed to build donor confidence.

**Inefficient Victim Application Management:** Existing platforms do not typically include systematic processes for victim application management, verification, and approval. There is a need for streamlined processes that ensure fair and efficient distribution of resources to those in need.

**Limited Integration with Local Mobile Money Services:** While mobile money services are widely used in Kenya, many platforms do not fully integrate with these services or optimize for mobile-first approaches. There is an opportunity to leverage existing mobile money infrastructure to enhance accessibility and convenience.

This project addresses these gaps by developing a comprehensive Community Support Management System specifically designed for the Kenyan context, incorporating CRM principles, mobile money integration, real-time tracking, and systematic victim application management.
